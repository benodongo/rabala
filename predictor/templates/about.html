history

Story

Started as field research documenting how elders read wind, moon, cloud and felt temperature.

Elicitation sessions across Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania were translated into a fuzzy rule base, then paired with calibrated ML on Open-Meteo data.

Today the platform serves a growing set of communities with daily forecasts.
